HUWA — isolated word recording, NOT a slice
-------------------------------------------
stage_huwa_word.mp3
  What: هُوَ on its own
  From: https://audio.qurancdn.com/wbw/112_001_002.mp3
        (quran.com / QuranWBW word-by-word audio; same file served by
         https://audios.quranwbw.com/words/112/112_001_002.mp3)
  Why not Husary: in Husary's murattal AND in his Muallim (teaching) recitation,
  "Qul huwa" is recited joined — there is no silence between the words, so every
  slice either kept Qul leftover at the start (?v=3) or cut the ه onset (?v=4).
  Both were rejected on live. This source is recorded word-by-word, so the clip
  needs no splice: XLSR decodes the whole encoded file as هُوَ with no ق/ل/ا.
  Processing: trim to speech, normalize to RMS 0.19 (ladder parity), 0.14s
  silence bookends, 20ms fades.
  Reciter is the word-by-word reciter, not al-Husary — do not label it Husary.

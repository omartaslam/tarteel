Why this matters now

AI programmes fail quietly when thinking quality is assumed.

Boards see adoption dashboards. Leaders feel activity. Risk and opportunity both hide in how people think when the tool is fluent and the information is incomplete.

Hiring & search

Stop asking “have you used AI?” Start asking whether candidates can judge it, challenge it, and redesign work around it.

L&D & talent

Train thinking strategies, not only tools. Your highest-ROI cohort is often strong thinkers who are under-leveraging AI.

Transformation leads

Usage is not readiness. Pair enablement with a clearer read on judgement, opportunity recognition and agency.
